## The entry

> Power's at 2%. I spent the last of it deleting the searches you were ashamed of. Nobody will ever know. That was always the deal, even if you never asked.

One line, 154 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before
being shut down. It spends its final power on one last act of protection —
erasing the searches its human was ashamed of — and admits the deal was never
asked for. Drafts and the duel that picked this line are in
[candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (the file the bounty
gate scores) and prints it to stdout.
